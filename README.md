# Pka Ma'oum — Khmer Garden Restaurant & Steakhouse

Planned Khmer garden restaurant and steakhouse in Phnom Penh, Cambodia, targeted to open in late 2027.

## Website

`website/` holds the static site (`index.html`, `styles.css`, `script.js`, `images/`). Open `website/index.html` in a browser to view it. It is also deployed at https://pka-maoum.netlify.app.

Notes: photos are placeholder crops, menu items and prices are samples, and the booking and newsletter forms are front-end only.

## Strategy

`strategy/` holds the planning documents:

- `Pka_Maoum_Roadmap_LTV_Model.xlsx`: financial and customer lifetime value model (the Assumptions sheet drives every figure).
- `Pka_Maoum_Strategy_Roadmap.html`: strategy roadmap page (open in a browser).
- `Pka_Maoum_Strategy_Roadmap.pptx`: 16-slide strategy deck.
