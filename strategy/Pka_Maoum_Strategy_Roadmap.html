LTV strategy

What each guest is worth

A blended guest is worth $155 in lifetime contribution against $7.90 to acquire. Expatriates are the most valuable segment, local families bring the volume, and tourists are won through free reviews, not paid ads.

LTV = annual spend × 60% contribution margin × (1 + 10%) ÷ (1 + 10% − annual retention). Customer = one guest. CAC = marketing and welcome-offer spend per new guest recorded in the CRM.

Eight levers that raise LTV

  1. Capture every guest. Phone or Telegram at every bill, linked to the loyalty app. 40% of bills at launch, 65% by late 2028.
  2. Bring new guests back within 90 days. A thank-you message next day and a 30-day return offer (free Khmer dessert or welcome drink). Repeat rate 25% at launch, 38% by late 2028.
  3. Raise visit frequency. Tuesday steak night, weekend Khmer family set menus, a monthly heritage dinner, Khmer New Year and Pchum Ben menus.
  4. Raise spend per visit. Servers recommend sides, sauces and drinks with every steak; sharing platters for groups. $10.50 → $11.50 from June 2028.
  5. Three-tier loyalty. Green: birthday dessert. Silver (5 visits in 6 months): 5% back, priority booking. Gold (12 visits a year): 10% back, a free garden-table booking, owner's table invitation. 800 members by Feb 2028, 3,000 by Dec 2028.
  6. Corporate accounts. Monthly invoicing, set lunches and private garden events for offices, embassies and NGOs. 15 active accounts by Dec 2028.
  7. Recover unhappy guests. Golden Rule 5: resolve at the table, log it, follow up next day. 95% resolved on the same visit.
  8. Tourists through reviews. QR review requests, hotel concierge partnerships at 10% commission, a small Khmer spice gift. Google 4.7+ with 400+ reviews by Dec 2028.
