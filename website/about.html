About Us

Our Story

A garden in Phnom Penh where Khmer cooking is treated as a living tradition, and every evening ends under the lanterns.

ផ្កាម្អម

The Name

Pka means flower. Ma'om is the fragrant rice-paddy herb that gives Khmer sour soups their lift. Together they name a small, everyday flower of the Cambodian table, and the spirit of our kitchen.

Our Concept

Living Khmer Cuisine & Craft Cocktails

Khmer food is a living tradition, not a museum piece. Our kitchen honours family and royal-court recipes, then cooks them with modern care: amok steamed in young coconut, samlor korko built on hand-pounded kroeung, Kampot pepper picked green on the coast.

Behind the bar, the Cambodian pantry leads: rice spirit infused with jasmine, palm sugar from Kampong Speu, red Kampot pepper, galangal and lemongrass. Over the charcoal, Australian and Wagyu beef is finished the Khmer way.

The Garden

An Evening Under the Trees

Pka Ma'oum is built around a garden: a carved wooden sala, a lotus pond, frangipani and brass lanterns. Lunch is bright and unhurried; dinner is candlelit, with golden hour at the bar every day from 17:00 to 19:00.

The garden terrace seats up to 60 guests and the private sala up to 20, for family celebrations, company dinners and receptions.

Our Philosophy
