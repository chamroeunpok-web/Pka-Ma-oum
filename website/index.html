Good to Know

Before You Visit

What is “living Khmer cuisine”?

It is how we describe our cooking: real Khmer recipes, from family kitchens to the royal court, prepared with modern technique and the best Cambodian ingredients. The flavours stay true; the presentation and craft move forward.

Do you cater for vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free guests?

Yes. Dishes are marked on the menu, and the kitchen can adapt most plates. Tell us about allergies when you book and remind your server on arrival.

Is there a dress code?

Smart casual. The garden is open-air with covered terraces, so light clothing is perfect for Phnom Penh evenings.

Can I come just for cocktails?

Of course. The garden bar welcomes walk-ins every evening, with Golden Hour from 17:00 to 19:00. Book ahead for groups of six or more.

Are children welcome?

Yes, at lunch and dinner. High chairs are available, just let us know when you book.

How do I pay?

We plan to accept US dollars, Cambodian riel, major credit cards and KHQR mobile payments. Prices are in US dollars and subject to 10% VAT and 5% service.
