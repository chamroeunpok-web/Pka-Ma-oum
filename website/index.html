Our Concept

A Taste of Cambodia,
A Journey of Flavors

Pka Ma'oum is a Khmer garden restaurant and steakhouse where tradition meets indulgence. Surrounded by lush greenery and a serene atmosphere, we bring you the authentic taste of Cambodian cuisine, complemented by expertly grilled premium steaks and international favorites.

Whether you are here for a casual meal, a special celebration, or a memorable evening with loved ones, Pka Ma'oum offers an unforgettable dining experience in the heart of Phnom Penh.
