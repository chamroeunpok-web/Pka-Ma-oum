Experiences

Every Occasion, Under the Trees

01

The Khmer Kitchen

Family recipes and royal-court classics: amok steamed in banana leaf, samlor korko stirred for hours, Kampot pepper crab. Cooked the way they should be, with herbs from local farms.

See the Khmer menu

02

The Charcoal Grill

Australian and local beef over glowing charcoal, finished with Kampot pepper or red wine jus. Join us every Tuesday for Steak Night, or pre-order the tomahawk to carve at the table.

See the steak menu

03

The Garden Bar

Signature cocktails built on Khmer rice spirit, lemongrass and jasmine, a short wine list chosen for the grill, and Happy Hour every evening from 17:00 to 19:00.

See the bar menu
